Analytics

We would like to use Google Analytics to understand which pages help visitors plan a trip on the Sognefjord, where people get stuck, and which activities they look at. Nothing is loaded before you decide, and we never use it for advertising.

Your choice is stored in this browser and can be changed later under “Analytics settings” in the footer. Read our privacy statement. If you would rather not be measured, you can and nothing will be loaded.
